# Crystal

Portfolio of a music and sound studio: the work it has made, and the recognition attached to that work.

## Language

### The portfolio

**Project**:
A piece the studio has made. It has a page when the home page or the work page lists it.
_Avoid_: work, case study, interior

**Project page**:
The page of a project listed on the home page or the work page.
_Avoid_: interior

**Home page**:
The page that presents the projects chosen for the front of the site.
_Avoid_: landing page

**Work page**:
The page that lists the projects chosen for the work section.
_Avoid_: projects page

**Archive**:
The page that lists the projects chosen for the archive.
_Avoid_: work list

**Info bubble**:
The title piece of the project player, which grows upward to show the description and credits.
_Avoid_: info panel, sidebar, drawer

### Recognition

**Award**:
One recognition line: a ceremony, a category, the work, and a year, with an optional image.
_Avoid_: nomination, prize

**Ceremony**:
The body that gives an award, such as the Academy Awards.
_Avoid_: award, festival, show

**Work**:
The piece named on an award.
_Avoid_: project

**Awards page**:
The page that lists awards in a drifting, freely scrollable column. Hover or focus on an award's text shows its image and pauses the drift.
_Avoid_: awards list, recognition page

### News

**News**:
The short updates shown in the header window (and again inside About).
_Avoid_: popup, announcement, banner

**News item**:
One update in News: a title, one or more pictures, and an optional link on the title.
_Avoid_: post, story, slide

### Merch

**Product**:
A thing the studio sells. It has a title, a price, a shop link, and pictures.
_Avoid_: item, merch item

**Merch page**:
The page that presents the products, each one in front of the next.
_Avoid_: shop, store

**Product page**:
The page of one product. Its pictures, price, and shop link belong to it.
_Avoid_: interior, detail

**Shop link**:
Where a visitor buys a product. It is offered on the product page.
_Avoid_: url, checkout
